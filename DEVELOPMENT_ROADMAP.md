# Development roadmap

1. Phase 0 established the application, documentation, Git workflow and validation commands.
2. Phase 1 delivered the responsive homepage, site shell and first company pages. Founder-approved company copy and leadership profiles remain open.
3. Phase 2 includes the official brand artwork, matte-black and champagne-gold palette, persistent global light and dark themes, and all seven product detail routes. Both themes are required for future work. Next, expand PaLoLabs, introduce Insights, and build dedicated InternHub and Connect introductions.
4. Phase 3 delivered eight browser-local Engineering Tools. Unit conversion remains a possible future tool.
5. Phase 4 delivered the PostgreSQL backend, enquiry flow and notification queue. Production activation requires provider configuration and smoke checks.
6. Phase 5 delivered staff authentication, enquiry management, audit history and outbox monitoring. Next, provision production staff, validate the deployment and complete the release checks.
